## Ongoing

**PaMuk-Bench.** A parallel benchmark for vision-language tasks in seven languages, with Radu Tudor Ionescu and a team of 13 researchers. I led the captioning study and built the method that taught three image generators six more languages, by distilling each model's own English behavior into its text encoder.
